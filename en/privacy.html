Privacy, in plain language.

The desktop application

MasQL stores the local project database and learning progress on your computer. It does not require an account and does not upload your SQL or exercise answers to this website.

This website

This static website has no added analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Fonts and screenshots are served from this site. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Downloads and support

Installers are hosted on GitHub Releases. Opening a download or creating an issue uses GitHub’s services and privacy terms. Please do not include private databases, passwords, or personal records in public issues.
